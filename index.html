About

I play hand drums the way a good conversation works: listen first, then answer.

Djembe is home base. I've played sidewalk circles, small bars, and long jams with friends, and I bring the same thing to every one of them: warmth, steady time, and a feel that makes the people around me play better. I'm looking for bands and artists who want that underneath their music.
